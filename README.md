# DEFEAT PIGGY MASTER!

A web game designed by Sara.

## Start the game

Double-click `index.html`. It will open in your web browser. No installation is needed.

## How to play

- Platy travels forward along a winding path automatically.
- The camera follows behind Platy.
- Use the left and right arrow keys to turn her while she runs forward.
- Platy runs forward automatically. Use the up arrow to make her jump.
- The stuffies sit on the dirt trail. Jump over them to scoop them up with Platy's moving tail.
- Caught stuffies appear on Platy's tail for a moment while she grows.
- Catch stuffies from the trail with Platy's tail to earn points and grow.
- Big stuffies are worth 1 point. Smaller, higher stuffies are harder to catch and worth 5 points.
- Steer around fruit. Touching fruit takes away 2 points.
- Each level randomly has rare powers or no powers at all. The player is not told ahead of time.
- When powers are included, they appear occasionally without becoming too common.
- Only three collectible stuffies appear at the same time, and Platy grows slowly.
- Each magnet gives one pull that brings in every stuffy currently on the trail. More magnets can appear later.
- Invisibility protects Platy from fruit for 15 seconds, but Piggy Master can still crush her.
- Mega Tail makes Platy's tail huge for 10 seconds so it can scoop up stuffies from farther away.
- The golden coin power instantly gives the player 50 saved coins.
- Trails of collectible coins sometimes appear while running and flying. Each collected coin adds 1 saved shop coin.
- Piggy Master falls every 20 seconds. Avoid her until Platy is bigger than she is.
- Higher levels increase the trail and fruit speed, add more fruit, and shorten Piggy Master's return timer. Difficulty increases are capped so later levels remain playable.
- If Piggy Master lands on a small Platy, Platy gets crushed.
- Failing restarts the same saved level without resetting the account, pets, coins, inventory, or care progress.
- Once Platy is bigger, move into Piggy Master's landing circle. Piggy Master will bounce off Platy and fly into space.
- Defeat Piggy Master to start the next level in a new setting.
- After defeating Piggy Master, double-tap the up arrow to fly forward into the clouds. If a pet is selected, Platy sits on its back and rides it.
- Search up to two clouds. Each searched cloud has a 40% chance of hiding a unicorn or dragon, but a pet needs an empty stable stall.
- Fly through the magic door to reach the shop. Half the level score, rounded up, becomes saved coins, and the level score resets.
- The shop's Home button saves the completed level and returns to the home screen. The next game starts on the next level.
- Buy unicorn food for 10 coins, dragon food for 10 coins, water for 15 coins, pet soap for 15 coins, or another stable stall for 10 coins. Each shop visit allows five purchases.
- Every successful shop purchase plays a cheerful bell ding.
- Named game accounts separately save levels, pets, coins, inventory, and pet care. Pets can become hungry, thirsty, or dirty; food, water, and soap care reset their need for 1–2 levels. A pet leaves after more than three completed rounds without the care it needs.
- When an account owns multiple pets, the selected flying pet automatically rotates to the next pet after every completed round.
- Every adopted pet receives its own saved color, shown while flying and in its stable stall.
- Every pet receives a saved name, and its name appears on a wooden plate above its stable stall. If Sara's name list is used up, the game invents more magical names without repeating one.
- The home-screen closet lets each account choose Platy's color, hat, and trail. More choices unlock after beating levels 1, 2, and 3.
- Every level changes to a different wild setting, including forests, jungles, snowy woods, crystal canyons, and volcano valleys.
- Every level becomes a little harder: the trail and fruit move faster, Piggy Master returns sooner, and Platy must grow slightly more. Extra fruit is added slowly on higher levels.
- The game has bouncy background music and special sounds for collecting, fruit, powers, earthquakes, and winning.
- Piggy Master's landings make a huge crash and leave cracks in the trail.
- Use the Sound button to turn all audio on or off.

## Project files

- `index.html` builds the page.
- `style.css` controls how the page looks.
- `game.js` contains the game rules.
- `assets/sprites/` contains a separate PNG picture for every character, stuffy, and fruit.
- Stuffy and fruit pictures are drawn with extra clear space around their edges so they do not look crowded or clipped.
- `assets/platy-riding-pets.png` shows Platy sitting on the backs of her flying pets.
- `assets/horse-stall-background.png` gives each pet a realistic wooden horse stall with iron hardware and straw.
